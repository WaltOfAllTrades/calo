# User complaints and praise: construction / feet-inch-fraction calculator apps

Research date: 2026-11-20. Method: WebSearch + WebFetch of store pages, aggregators and forums; Google Play
read through the browser pane because WebFetch only returns Play's navigation bar. Quotes are verbatim and under
25 words. Dates are as shown on the source page ("Jul 23" with no year means the page did not show one).

## 4. Praise worth copying

- Being on the phone you already carry: "My phone is always with me and now so is my Construction Master." (justuseapp);
  "Having it on my phone is so incredibly convenient!!" (mwm.ai 2026-06-24). The single most repeated positive.
- Hold-a-key help: "the hold-a-key help function" and "the long-press info feature" (BuildCalc iOS 2024); CM5 "Pressing and
  holding any button opens a Help menu with a real-world example" (Family Handyman). Both market leaders do this; users name it.
- Triple keypad for whole / numerator / denominator (Fraction Calculator Plus, 205K Android reviews at 4.9): "This concept is
  beautifully realized" (2022-04-04); "I can't get over how simple it is." (iOS 2022-03-30). Also "correct them when missed type".
- Full expression kept on screen after equals (xNeat Fraction Calculator 2016; Fraction Calculator Plus step-by-step).
- Showing the result in several formats at once: fraction and decimal together (FCP), split screen with decimal, feet/inch,
  memory and scaling (Construction Calc listing), unit cycling (Measure Master Pro 2020).
- Results as a list after one or two taps, instead of pressing the same key repeatedly (BuildCalc positioning; its 4.7
  ratings on both stores and "Most intuitive const app out there" suggest it lands).
- One-time, upfront price: "Thank you for charging a fair and upfront price!" (Foots 2025); "buy buildcalc it's a one time
  purchase with the same function" (CM Pro Play 2025). A fair price is itself a praised feature.
- Accessible developer: BuildCalc's "The developer is accessible and open to ideas" (49 helpful, 2023); Calculated
  Industries' tech support gets named thank-yous even inside the subscription backlash (2024-05-30, 2023-11-28).
- Offline, no account, no data collection: Chippy "one offline database"; Handyman Calculator and BuildCalc show
  "No data collected" on Play. Asked for in the words "remote job sites" and "no service".
- Key-click sound that is easy to turn off (Measure Master Pro 2022) and dark/terminal themes (Winograd 2019 reply).

## 5. Opportunities (gaps no app in this set fills)

1. A free, no-ads, no-subscription feet-inch-fraction calculator with a credible pro-grade engine. Every 4.7+ app is
   either subscription (CM family, Construction Calculator Master, Chippy), ad-funded with upsell (xNeat, FCP, Winograd),
   or $20-25 one-time (BuildCalc). The low-cost one-time apps (Foots, Workman's, inchCALC) are praised for honesty but
   lack history, parentheses, backspace or modern layouts. Forty-nine complaints say "it's a calculator" should not rent.
2. Entry that needs no unit-marking ritual and forgives mistakes: type "5 ' 3 1/2" naturally, backspace through a
   fraction, see the whole expression, parentheses. The only app praised for solving input (inchCALC+, 2023) has 17
   ratings and an iPhone-4-era layout.
3. Precision made visible, not a hidden setting: show the rounding denominator next to the answer and let the user tap
   it (1/16 default, 1/32, 1/64, exact). Four complaints and the Sawmill Creek thread come from users who thought a
   1/16-rounded answer was a bug. Also offer decimal feet and mm as one-tap read-outs (items 89-90).
4. State that survives the screen lock, app switch and reinstall: a paper tape / history with tap-to-reuse is asked for by
   name in three small apps and praised in BuildCalc; "screen shutting off" is the top non-price complaint on CM Pro iOS.
5. Jobsite readability as a first-class mode: large keys, high contrast, dark and daylight themes, Dynamic Type. BuildCalc
   ships a "Full Daylight Visibility Mode" and FieldLab notes no competitor has dark mode. Evidence for gloves/sunlight is
   indirect (hardware reviews only), so validate with field testing rather than assume.
6. Apple Watch and iPad landscape: each asked for once and shipped by no one in this set. Cheap to be first.
7. Honest free tier: the loudest non-CM anger is "free" apps that lock the plus key or add ads after purchase. A free app
   that stays free, with no locked keys, has a clear positioning line against items 26, 33-41.
8. Offline by construction: no receipt validation that needs a connection, no ad download before first use. Say it in the
   listing in the users' words ("works with no signal, on remote job sites").
9. Teach without the manual: hold-a-key help and worked examples are the praised learning aid in both leaders; the
   apprenticeship-instructor objection (FHB 2009) suggests showing the working, as Fraction Calculator Plus does, is a
   differentiator for trainees.
10. Metric parity for Canadian/Australian/UK users: mm key without an extra step, metric in material estimators. Three
    complaints, no app praised for it except Megabyte's (3.3 stars).

## 6. Caveats

- Apple shows four reviews per listing, chosen by Apple; Google Play shows three plus a "See all" list sorted by relevance.
  Counts are therefore of what is visible, not of all reviews. mwm.ai's 669-review histogram for CM Pro iOS is the only
  near-complete distribution obtained.
- Vendor pages (FieldLab, getconstructioncalculator.com) and affiliate pages (consumerability.com) are cited only where the
  claim is specific; their rankings are biased by design.
- Reddit, Amazon, Home Depot, ContractorTalk, LumberJocks and Sawmill Creek were not readable; anything attributed to them is a
  search-snippet paraphrase and is flagged unverified. Re-running this with a human browser session would add hardware
  ergonomics (gloves, sunlight, key feel) which this report could not source directly.
